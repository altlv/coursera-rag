Angular documentation assistant

Ask questions about Angular, answered from the real docs

A retrieval-augmented generation prototype. Questions are matched against a local copy of the Angular documentation, and the most relevant passages are handed to a language model that answers from them and cites what it used.

The assistant is docked on the right. It stays with you as you browse.

How it works

  1. 1

    Scrape

    Angular docs pages are downloaded to local JSON.

    scripts/fetch-angular-docs.js
  2. 2

    Chunk

    Pages are split into passages small enough to be mostly signal.

    server/rag.js
  3. 3

    Embed

    Each passage becomes a vector capturing its meaning.

    server/build-vector-store.js
  4. 4

    Retrieve

    The question is embedded too, then compared against every passage.

    selectChunks()
  5. 5

    Generate

    The best passages become context for a grounded, cited answer.

    generateAnswer()
